Research & capability notes

Prepared September 8, 2026, for Paddy Jay’s private experimental sky-observation app.

No reliable UAP-attraction frequency was established by the sources reviewed. The app supports inspectable experiments and records observations; it does not authenticate extraterrestrial contact. Audio frequency, pulse repetition rate and optical wavelength are different quantities.

What was investigated

Primary qCode manufacturer and partner materials, Greer’s official app/manual/transcript, Chilbolton reconstructions and field analysis, astronomical software and camera documentation. Google Drive searches covered Jackson, qCode/Q-code, CE5, Greer and crop-circle terms. Readable retrieval of JBIS.pdf concerned broader visitation arguments; Linkedin_Posts_2024_Blue.pdf contained software articles, not a qCode specification. No exact signal specification was recovered from these Drive results.

Chilbolton data provenance

Exact 6-pixel grid extraction from IdS’s public-domain published reconstruction; one explicit border cell removed on each side. Every cell’s 25 interior pixels was checked for uniform black/white. No bits interpolated. Binary SHA-256: 6eb11753b32702e0a0eecde6ca8e837e4989e43ff515bbef5ff72548393708bc.

This verifies the transcription of that reconstruction, not the original field. Johannes Rössel’s alternate SVG has 74 rows and was not silently forced to 73. Crabwood’s 2002 ASCII spiral is a different formation. Download bits and provenance.

Field limitations

  • Calibrate heading, elevation and field of view against known stars. Compass interference, device tilt, video crop and refraction can displace labels.
  • The catalog is finite. An unmatched point may be aircraft, satellite, meteor, cloud, noise, reflection, lens flare or another ordinary source.
  • Bright-point assistance uses a threshold and simple camera-motion rejection. It is not AI anomaly classification, and tracking can jump to a different bright point.
  • Video is raw camera image with generated/imported audio when available, no AR labels or microphone. Five-minute clips limit memory use; this is not a background recorder.
  • Stop All and backgrounding stop output. Real flashlight latency and thermal behavior have not been measured. Screen brightness changes patch opacity, not calibrated luminous intensity.
  • Flashing can trigger photosensitive seizures. Optional rates are bounded but not certified safe. Do not direct light toward aircraft, traffic or people.
  • Local browser storage can be evicted by the operating system. Export important media and logs. Offline operation requires a successful first load/cache; private-site sign-in can affect relaunch.
  • Native kit is unsigned and not Xcode/device-tested here. Camera, export, offline storage and torch behavior require real iPhone acceptance testing.

Sources

qCode

Manufacturer specifies patterned strobe and continuous/burst controls but no exact timing, bits or wavelengths. No authentic qCode payload is bundled.

qCode Android

Product description supplies claims rather than a numerical protocol.

CE5 manual

Printed page 97 describes three flashes, listening, then four flashes. Pulse durations and waits are app-defined. No proprietary audio is copied.

CE5 visualization transcript

Original guidance paraphrases the high-level practice. Proposed consciousness-mediated contact is not established by these materials.

Chilbolton reconstruction

IdS, 2010, public domain (PD-self). 23×73 bitmap. 428 ones; 1,251 zeros. White=1, left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Alternative reconstructions differ.

Primary Chilbolton analysis

Paul Vigay reports field investigation on August 25, 2001. This is historical interpretation, not verified extraterrestrial authorship.

Astronomy Engine

Don Cross, MIT. Local planetary ephemerides and precession. Source license is retained in lib/astronomy.js.

D3-Celestial

Olaf Frohn. Hipparcos-based magnitude-six catalog and constellation geometry. BSD-3-Clause notice is bundled.

Satellite.js

SGP4 orbital propagation, MIT. A predicted above-horizon position does not establish illumination, visibility, or detection.

CelesTrak

Optional orbital elements. Updates requested only on user action. Epochs older than seven days are suppressed.

Apple torch interface

Torch brightness and mode are device capabilities, not a selectable optical wavelength.

Capacitor iOS

Native build kit uses Capacitor 8 and requires Xcode 26+. Signing and on-device testing remain owner-dependent.
